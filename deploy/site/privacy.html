xalarm privacy policy

Last updated: July 2026

Short version: xalarm collects no personal data. Your alarms, schedules, and settings never leave your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Alarms, shift schedules, world-clock cities, stopwatch/timer state, imported alarm sounds, and app settings are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Data the app transmits

  • Nothing by default. The core app works fully offline.
  • Time Sync (optional): if you start a sync session, the display name you type, a temporary session code, and timer/stopwatch actions (start/stop times) are relayed through the sync server to the one person you explicitly pair with. Sessions are held in the server's memory only, are never written to disk, and are deleted when the session ends.
  • Home Assistant API (optional, off by default): when you enable it, the app answers requests on your own local network, protected by a token you control. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Sounds added from a URL (optional): the file is downloaded once directly from the URL you provide and stored on your device.

Data we collect

None. We operate no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no user accounts. We cannot see your alarms, schedules, or usage.

Permissions the app requests

  • Alarms & reminders / notifications: to ring alarms reliably at exact times.
  • Sensors (accelerometer): used only on-device for the Zen hourglass tilt controls.
  • Files (when you choose a sound): only the audio file you pick is copied into the app.

Children

xalarm is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address. Questions: open an issue on the project repository.
