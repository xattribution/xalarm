v1.5.0 (first Play release) — paste into "What's new" (500 char max):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
First release.

Alarms that follow rotating shift schedules — Panama (2-2-3), DuPont,
Pitman, 4-on/4-off, or build your own cycle, with different start times
on different days. Plus a world clock with real city search, a stopwatch
that survives restarts, timers, a zen hourglass focus timer you control
by tilting the phone, home screen widgets, and a live shared timer you
can pair with one other person. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
----------------------------------------------------------------------

v1.6.0
- Time Sync now holds up to eight phones. Whoever you join is the host,
  who can let people in, lock controls so the rest only watch, and remove
  members. Share a session as a QR code or scan one to join.
- Fixed snoozed alarms being dropped when the app restarted or the alarm
  was edited.
- Fixed one-time and "stop after N times" alarms staying switched on after
  their last ring.
- Fixed repeating alarms going quiet after being stopped from the
  notification many times without opening the app.
- One-time alarms can now be set for a specific date.
----------------------------------------------------------------------
